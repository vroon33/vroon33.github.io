    !! This rasterises every page. Each page is re-rendered as a JPEG image, so text stops being selectable, searchable or copyable, and any existing OCR/text layer is discarded. Good for scans, photos and image-heavy decks — a bad idea for text documents, forms you need to search, or anything that will be read by a parser.
